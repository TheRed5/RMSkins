The following instructions may be slightly out of date. Just consult your local search engine or friendly AI agent to shore it up.

🔑 How to Get Your OpenWeatherMap API Key
1. Create an OpenWeather Account
Go to the official signup page:
home.openweathermap.org/users/sign_up
Fill in your email, password, and accept the terms.

2. Verify Your Email
After signing up, OpenWeather sends a confirmation email.
Your first API key (called APPID) is generated automatically and included in that email.

3. Go to “My API Keys”
Once logged in, visit:
home.openweathermap.org/api_keys
Here you can:
- View your default API key
- Generate additional keys
- Rename or delete keys

You can find your OpenWeatherMap City ID by searching for your city on the OpenWeatherMap Find Page or downloading their global JSON list.
Follow these exact steps to get your City ID:
1. Go to the OpenWeatherMap Find Page.
2. Type your city name into the search bar and click Search.
3. Click on the correct city from the list of results.
4. Look at the URL in your web browser's address bar. The number at the end of the URL is your City ID (e.g., openweathermap.org/city/5128581 means your ID is 5128581).
